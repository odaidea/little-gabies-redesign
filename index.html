Our story

Wrapped in heritage. Made with heart.

Little Gabies began with a handmade Ethiopian Gabi blanket passed down through generations — a gift that carried the warmth of family and tradition.

That inspiration became a double-layered handwoven cotton blanket for babies, with delicate embroidery and a commitment to natural materials. Today the collection celebrates Ethiopian artisanship through baby footwear, scarves, and thoughtful everyday essentials.
